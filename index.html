What TechBridge is

TechBridge is a learning and internship initiative from Baselink Services Limited. Its purpose is simple: help learners connect what they study with the practical work they will meet in the real world.

Who it is for

The programs are for learners and aspiring interns who want hands-on experience in Data Analytics or Web Development, whether you are just starting out or ready to put your skills to work.

Programs available now

Choose the track that fits your goals. Both are delivered through the 30-day practical internship.

Data Analytics

Learn to work with data and turn it into useful insights through practical, project-based tasks.

Web Development

Learn to plan, build and style real web pages through hands-on projects.

The 30-day practical internship

Instead of only reading about skills, you practise them. The internship is project-based, so you learn by building things you can be proud of.

  • Learn by doing. Practical tasks and projects replace passive lessons.
  • Possible recommendation letter. Interns who participate successfully and perform well may receive a recommendation letter.
  • Two tracks. Pick Data Analytics or Web Development.
  • A community behind you. Connect with other learners on WhatsApp.
